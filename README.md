# 📊 Blinkit Power BI Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** that provides comprehensive analysis of Blinkit's (India's Last Minute App) sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution. The dashboard delivers actionable insights across key business metrics to support data-driven decision making.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

This dashboard was built to analyze Blinkit's grocery delivery data and uncover trends in sales, customer ratings, outlet performance, and item-level analytics. It enables stakeholders to quickly identify top-performing categories, outlet types, and geographic trends.

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| **Total Sales** | Overall revenue generated from all items sold |
| **Average Sales** | Average revenue per sale |
| **Number of Items** | Total count of different items sold |
| **Average Rating** | Average customer rating for items sold |

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features & Visualizations

### Charts & Analysis

- **Total Sales by Fat Content** — Donut Chart analyzing the impact of fat content (Low Fat vs Regular) on total sales, along with other KPI variations by fat content.
- **Total Sales by Item Type** — Bar Chart identifying the performance of different item categories and their contribution to total sales.
- **Fat Content by Outlet for Total Sales** — Stacked Column Chart comparing sales across outlets segmented by fat content.
- **Total Sales by Outlet Establishment Year** — Line Chart evaluating how the age or type of outlet establishment influences total sales.
- **Sales by Outlet Size** — Donut/Pie Chart analyzing the correlation between outlet size (Small, Medium, High) and total sales.
- **Sales by Outlet Location** — Funnel Map visualizing the geographic distribution of sales across different location tiers (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3).
- **All Metrics by Outlet Type** — Matrix Card providing a comprehensive view of all key metrics broken down by outlet type (Grocery Store vs Supermarket Types).
